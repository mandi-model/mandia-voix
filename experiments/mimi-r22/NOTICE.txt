Mandia Voix Mimi r22 — experimental research artifacts.

Free download for inspection and research. No production-quality, naturalness,
validated Ivorian-accent or state-of-the-art claim. Third-party components retain
their original terms; this package does not license third-party source recordings.
The platform entry uses "Usage restreint" pending a documented unified weight
license; do not infer an unrestricted commercial license from a free download.

Mimi codec and codebook-derived initialization: Kyutai, CC BY 4.0.
https://huggingface.co/kyutai/mimi
Revision: 89091b3e466eb6a9d11e537bf26b144f194978f7
Moshi paper: https://kyutai.org/Moshi.pdf
Our predictor reuses our N lexical encoder and initialized codebook embeddings;
it is not an entirely random-init or entirely Mandia-pretrained system.

French corpus: SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database, 2016, Yamagishi,
Honnet, Garner and Lazaridis, University of Edinburgh (2017).
https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/1705 — CC BY 4.0 as documented by the corpus.
Resampling, automatic alignment and preprocessing are modifications.
IvoireTTS: https://dataset.mandia.org/gnakalehacker/ivoiretts-v0-1
The small CI adaptation subset comes from one source; the source example's
accent/cleanup received listener feedback, not a full verbatim/speaker audit.
Neither the source recordings nor the user's personal test voice are included.
